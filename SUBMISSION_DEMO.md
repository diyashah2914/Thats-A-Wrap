# 🏆 Submission Demo Runbook

## 3-minute story

### 1. Set the premise

> “That's a Wrap! treats filmmaking like a production system. AI understands the script and makes structured creative decisions; deterministic media workers execute those decisions; QC and human approval keep the filmmaker in control.”

### 2. Create a project

- Projects → New Project
- Enter film name
- Paste a short screenplay
- Create Project

Show the **10 scenes generated by the Director Agent**.

### 3. Upload real footage

- Open Scene 01
- Upload an MP4/MOV
- Show that the scene now has footage

### 4. Process

Click **Process Scene**.

Explain:

```text
Editor Agent
   ↓
Specialist plans
   ↓
FFmpeg worker
   ↓
QC Agent
```

### 5. Show the real output

Open Review Scenes and play the actual generated MP4.

### 6. Human-in-the-loop

Approve the scene.

Say:

> “AI can recommend; the filmmaker still controls what becomes part of the final cut.”

### 7. Assemble

Approve additional processed scenes and click **Assemble Final Film**.

Play the final assembled output.

### 8. Show the control room

Open Grafana and show:

- job counts
- completed / failed jobs
- agent calls
- processing time

### 9. Recovery moment

If you have time, demonstrate a failed job and show the Production Agent's recovery path.

## What not to claim

Do not claim that the 9-day build is a Hollywood-grade autonomous editor. The honest and strong claim is:

> “This is a working AI-assisted production pipeline that turns structured creative decisions into real media processing, QC, approval and final assembly.”
